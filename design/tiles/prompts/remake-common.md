# Remake of 6 service pages (16 Nov): common composition

tile at 460px tall. So the device must be MEDIUM-LARGE and sit between 45% and 82% of the frame
width (never wider), top edge at 38% of the frame height, with empty background on both sides
of it and above it. Everything sharp (f/11), no bokeh, blur, haze or vignette. Blank flat white
screens, no reflections. No faces, no text, no logos (except printed items described).

and above it, and nothing in front of the screen. The [device] screen is completely blank, flat
pure white, evenly lit, no content, no reflections. No people's faces, no text, no logos."
