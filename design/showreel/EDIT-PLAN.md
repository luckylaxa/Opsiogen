The plan for the final film. Lucky's decisions are recorded here so the final edit can follow them without
asking again. **Nothing is rendered until Lucky approves the Figma artboards.** The final edit runs on Fable
(few credits left there), so everything is prepared here first: the plan, the shot list, the stock picks and
the artboards.

**Motion and transitions they use** (to use our own way, never copied):
- Word-by-word type on flat colour, with one italic serif word for emphasis.
- Match cuts: a blank device becomes a designed screen; motion continues across cuts.
- Slide-over: the next shot enters from a corner over a still word card.
- Circle wipe from an element growing to fill the frame.
- Grid re-shuffle, and grid zoom: a mosaic tightens into one tile.
- Mirror (kaleidoscope) frame, one beat only.
- Speed ramps and whip pans with motion blur between real footage.
- Billboards and screens in real places, each held about half a second.
- Fastest cutting in the last third, then a calm, typed ending.
- Colour: one flat colour per section, chapter words alone on that colour.

## Cut 7 (18 Nov): our own motion, with our character

Lucky on cut six: "not happy with animation. its just slide animation. first version animation was far better",
then "check superside video as well" and "dont have to copy same. we can have our own style and dont forget to add
our character intraction". Cut 7 keeps cut six's 103 shots, stock and music and changes only the motion.

- **The spring (cut five's feel).** Every piece springs in fast (0.2 s, a little overshoot) and keeps drifting;
  pairs, phones, browsers and the pieces of a collage each take their own entrance.
- **The lens (our circle).** The character's eye is a camera, so our circle is its lens: the opening clip and each
  chapter card open in a crisp growing circle; after "We take it from here." the camera dives into its eye and the
  burst opens out of its pupil.
- **The hop.** It hops along each chapter line on the eighths and stamps the words in, springs out of the top, and
  falls into the next scene beside the people (the cheering man after Brand, the woman on blue after Design). On
  Brand / Design / Build / Launch it bounces once a beat.
- **The touch.** It zips through the burst grid and the work changes in its wake.
- **The look.** It pops up beside the woman with the idea and turns to her; it watches the closing lines type (a nod
  for each word, eyes following the lines), then turns to us and winks; it lands with the logo, reads the call and
  blinks. Only on plain studio backdrops, never pasted into real scenes (office, jeep, home).
- **Solid (cut 8).** Lucky: "It's solid character so you can't change the shape of the character ... may be you can
  try rotation while landing or settle." It never squashes or stretches: it spins as it drops in, leans into a hop,
  rocks on landing until it settles, rolls as it zips through the grid and turns into place as it pops up.
- **Cut 9: cut 5's moves on the approved shots.** Lucky on cut 8: "Still character 360 rotation/spinning is missing
  and many of the v5 animations are missing. Missing snappy, bouncy type of creative animation." Then: "Don't use old
  files. Use only latest figma files, only change in animation" and "on landing or still phase we can rotate the
  character and blink eye ... Don't have to rotate all the time." So the 103 approved Figma shots stay exactly as they
  are (the stills match) and only the motion changes: every entrance overshoots more (pop 2.5) and tilts in and wobbles
  into place; boards of four or more pieces (Torvi, Vaelo, SandLogic) and the burst grid come in as cut 5's tilted wall
