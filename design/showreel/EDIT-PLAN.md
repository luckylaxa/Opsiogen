# Opsiogen showreel: edit plan (fresh, 18 Nov 2026)

The plan for the final film. Lucky's decisions are recorded here so the final edit can follow them without
asking again. **Nothing is rendered until Lucky approves the Figma artboards.** The final edit runs on Fable
(few credits left there), so everything is prepared here first: the plan, the shot list, the stock picks and
the artboards.

## Lucky's decisions (18 Nov)

- **Approved work, as a mix:** the service-page artwork (gradient tiles), our sample designs (Maison Avelle,
  Oriel, Torvi, the Vaelo car), Afthab, plus the client work in the Figma files he listed.
- **Websites:** rebuild clean website artboards from the real site captures (the site films in
  `public/work/<slug>/<slug>-flow.mp4`), not the case study mockups.
- **Shot list:** fresh, not cut five's.
- **Stock video:** yes, free Adobe Stock clips through the Adobe connector, to tie the work to real moments,
  as the reference does. **Only professionally shot clips with one or two people. Nothing crowded or rushed.**
- **No case study gallery images with their backgrounds**, and nothing customised only by cropping: every
  artboard is built for the 16:9 film from the original work.
- **No redesign of client screens** (no recoloured versions).
- **No hero concept renders** (character line-up, rainbow ribbon, lobby scenes). New illustration only if made
  for the reel.
- Cut five stays the current version until the new film is approved.

**Motion and transitions they use** (to use our own way, never copied):
- Word-by-word type on flat colour, with one italic serif word for emphasis.
- Match cuts: a blank device becomes a designed screen; motion continues across cuts.
- Slide-over: the next shot enters from a corner over a still word card.
- Circle wipe from an element growing to fill the frame.
- Grid re-shuffle, and grid zoom: a mosaic tightens into one tile.
- Mirror (kaleidoscope) frame, one beat only.
- Speed ramps and whip pans with motion blur between real footage.
- Billboards and screens in real places, each held about half a second.
- Fastest cutting in the last third, then a calm, typed ending.
- Colour: one flat colour per section, chapter words alone on that colour.

**Rules for the artboards (Figma, one per shot):**
- 1920 x 1080. Our flat colours only behind the work. No case study backgrounds, no shadows on flat mockups.
- The work large and sharp: never scaled above its own size; product screens exported at 2x.
- Phones and screens placed for the motion they will have (where they enter, where they stop).
- Stock clips get their own artboard with a still from the clip and a note of the in and out points.
- Type: Outfit, our sizes; one italic emphasis word allowed in the opening line.
- Client names never on screen. Torvi, Vaelo, Maison Avelle and Oriel are our design work and are never
  labelled as clients.

## Figma artboards

**Style, after Lucky's review (18 Nov): like the reference, full-bleed photography.** Our designs live on
real devices and objects in real places (a phone in a hand, a tablet held up, a laptop on a desk, print on a
table), the way the Vaelo car frame does. No flat cards floating on a colour. Flat colour only for the single
chapter word and the small opening type. `build/screen_fit.py` puts a screen onto a photo's blank screen in
perspective (scene photos from Unsplash in `raw/scenes`, results in `raw/comps`). Page "00 · Samples in the
reference style" holds the samples; page "x · First try" was rejected.
