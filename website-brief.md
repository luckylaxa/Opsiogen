# Opsiogen Website: Content Structure & Build Plan

Oct 7, 2026 · \@Lucky

The Opsiogen website is work-led and minimal: big project images and
video, very little text. It has five main pages plus templates for
project and service pages, all editable in a CMS. The design follows
your screenshots and screen recording.

The footer also links to a Privacy Policy text page, and a simple \"page

**The rule for every page:** projects carry the story. Text stays to a
heading and a line or two.

**Adding pages later:** create a new page in the CMS from the sections
the site already uses, and publish. No code needed.

## Home

Five sections. Work takes most of the page.

1.  **Hero.** Small label: AI-first design and digital company. Heading:
    **Your digital department, without the hiring.** Button: Start a
    project.

2.  **Featured project.** One large image or video, with the project
    name, client, service tags and a View project link. You pick which
    project in the CMS.

3.  **Latest work.** Six project cards (image, project name, client,
    service tag) and a View all work link.

4.  **Services.** Heading: **Our services.** The eight service names
    with their taglines, in three groups (Build, Grow, Create), each
    linking to its page.

5.  **Call to action.** Heading: **One partner. Every digital need. One
    monthly plan.** Button: Start a project.

The call-to-action block is shared by every page and edited in one
place.

## Work

### Work page (/work)

-   Heading: **Work**

-   Text: Selected projects across websites, products, content and
    print.

-   Filter chips: All · Websites · Product Design · MVPs & Apps ·
    Automation · Social Media · Digital Marketing · Creative · Print

-   Project grid: cover image or video, project name, client, service
    tags. Shows 12 projects, then a Load more button.

-   Call to action: the shared block.

### Project page template (/work/\[project-name\])

1.  **Title.** Project name; client, industry and year; service tags;
    optional Visit site link.

2.  **Cover.** Full-width image or video.

3.  **Summary.** Two or three lines on what we did.

4.  **Result (optional).** One number and a label, only if you have a
    real result to show.

5.  **Gallery.** Full-width and side-by-side images of the work (desktop
    and mobile screens, print, social posts).

6.  **Next project.** Large link to the next project.

7.  **Call to action.** The shared block.

**Before launch:** add at least six projects (nine is better) so Home
and Work look full. Each one needs a cover image or short video, four to
eight gallery images, and its name, client, industry, services, year and
a two-to-three-line summary.

### AI Websites & Revamps

We design and build fast, conversion-focused websites with an
easy-to-use CMS, so you can update content without waiting on
developers. Built with AI and finished by designers, your site goes from
brief to launch in days, not months.

Includes: new websites, revamps of outdated sites, landing pages, SaaS
and product sites, corporate and portfolio sites.

Note on page: *The 7-day timeline applies to basic websites.*

### AI Product Design & Design Systems

We design SaaS, enterprise and mobile products that are simple to use
and quick to adopt. AI speeds up research, flows and screens, and our
designers make every decision count. Design systems keep your product
consistent as it grows from MVP to enterprise.

Includes: UX audits, product redesigns, new product UX/UI, mobile app
design, design systems, developer-ready handoff.

### AI MVPs & Business Apps

We turn ideas into working software fast: MVPs to test with users and
investors, portals for your customers, and internal tools that replace
spreadsheets and manual work.

Includes: MVPs and prototypes, customer and partner portals, dashboards,
booking and intake systems, internal tools and trackers.

### AI Automation

We find the repetitive tasks slowing your business down and automate
them with AI, from lead handling and document processing to reports and
approvals. Your tools work together, and your people get hours back
every week.

Includes: process audit, lead and enquiry automation, document and data
extraction, AI chatbots, report and proposal generation, approval
workflows, monthly care plan.

### AI Social Media Marketing

We plan, create and publish your social content every month: posts,
reels, AI video and captions in your brand voice. You get consistent
output, faster turnaround and a clear monthly report.

Includes: content calendar, post and carousel design, reels and AI
video, captions, scheduling and publishing, performance reporting.

### Digital Marketing

We run paid campaigns and search visibility aimed at what matters:
enquiries, leads and return on ad spend.

Includes: Meta, Google and LinkedIn ads, lead generation campaigns, SEO,
analytics and reporting.

### AI Creative Subscription

Dedicated creative support on a flat monthly subscription. Send requests
any time and we deliver on-brand work fast, without hiring in-house or
briefing a new agency for every project.

Includes: ad creatives, presentations and pitch decks, brand assets,
motion graphics, AI imagery and video, email and landing page design.

### AI Print & Collateral Design

We design brochures, catalogues, packaging and event collateral with AI
speed and designer precision. Every file is checked and delivered ready
for print.

Includes: brochures, catalogues, flyers, posters, packaging and labels,
stationery, expo booths and banners.

## How to build it

Claude Code builds the whole site from one prompt, using this doc for
content and your screenshots and recording for design. You review it on
your computer, then publish.

**Have these ready**

-   [ ] Opsiogen logo as SVG, light and dark versions

-   [ ] Brand colour hex codes, if you want them on buttons and
    highlights

-   [ ] Full-page screenshots of each reference page, desktop and mobile

-   [ ] Screen recordings of the animations: scroll, hover, menu and
    page changes

-   [ ] At least six projects: a cover image or short video, four to
    eight gallery images, and the details listed under Work (you can
    also add these in the CMS after the build)

-   [ ] Contact email, phone and social links

-   [ ] Free accounts: GitHub, Sanity (sanity.io, the content editor)
    and Resend (resend.com, sends form emails)

**Steps**

1.  Make a folder called opsiogen-website. Inside it, add a
    design-reference folder with the screenshots and recordings, and a
    brand folder with the logo and colours. Optionally, add a projects
    folder with one subfolder of images per project. If you\'re starting
    from your website template repo, use a copy of it as this folder.

2.  Save this doc into the folder as website-brief.md.

3.  Open Claude Code in the folder and paste the prompt below. It asks
    for your account keys when it needs them.

4.  Review the site at the local link Claude Code gives you, and ask for
    changes in plain words.

5.  Push to GitHub and connect your host. Hostinger runs this site only
    on plans with Node.js web apps (Business or Cloud)
    ([Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/support/how-to-deploy-a-nodejs-website-in-hostinger)).
    If your plan doesn\'t have that, use Vercel Pro, because Vercel\'s
    free plan is for non-commercial use only
    ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines)).

6.  Point your domain to the host, then edit content at
    yourdomain.com/studio.

**Claude Code prompt**

Build the Opsiogen company website.\
\
INPUTS IN THIS FOLDER\
- website-brief.md: pages, sections and content. Use its wording
exactly. Keep text short. Do not add pages, sections, services or claims
that are not in it.\
- design-reference/: screenshots and screen recordings. Match this
design exactly: layout, grid, spacing, type sizes and weights, colours,
project cards, menu, footer, and every animation and hover effect. The
site is work-led: big project images and video, very little text.\
- brand/: Opsiogen logo (SVG) and brand colours.\
- projects/ (optional): one subfolder per project with its images and a
short notes file.\
\
For the recordings, extract frames with ffmpeg (about 4 per second
around each interaction) and study them to recreate each animation:
trigger, direction, timing and easing.\
Do not copy any logo, image, icon or text from the references; use only
their layout and motion. If they use a paid font, use the closest free
Google Font. Use neutral placeholder images where I have not supplied
project images.\
If this folder already contains a Next.js project, build on it.
Otherwise create a new one.\
\
STACK (keep it minimal)\
- Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) + Tailwind CSS\
- Sanity as the CMS, with the editing studio at /studio\
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger for animations; add Lenis only if the recording
shows smooth scrolling\
- Resend for contact form emails\
- Standard Next.js app deployed from GitHub (Hostinger Node.js web app
or Vercel)\
\
PAGES\
Home (/), Work (/work), project pages (/work/\[slug\]) from one
template, Services (/services), 8 service pages (/services/\[slug\])
from one template, About (/about), Contact (/contact), Privacy Policy
(/privacy, placeholder text), 404. Sections exactly as in
website-brief.md.\
\
CMS (everything editable without a developer)\
- Site settings: logo, footer line, contact email/phone, social links,
shared call-to-action block (heading + button), featured project for
Home, default SEO and share image.\
- Project: name, slug, client, industry, year, services (references),
cover image or video, short summary, optional result (number + label),
gallery images, optional live link, order, SEO.\
- Service: name, slug, group (Build/Grow/Create), short filter name,
order, tagline, description, included tags, optional note, SEO.\
- Page (Home, Work, Services, About, Contact, Privacy and future pages):
title, slug, SEO, and a list of sections editors can add, remove and
reorder.\
- Enquiry (read-only in the studio): contact form submissions.\
- Section types: hero, featured project, project grid, services list,
short text, promise list, call to action.\
- Every image requires alt text. Published changes appear on the live
site within 60 seconds (time-based revalidation).\
- Seed script: load all text from website-brief.md, the projects in
projects/ if present, otherwise 6 placeholder projects I can replace in
the studio.\
\
WORK\
- Work page: project grid with filter chips (All + the 8 services by
short name); show 12 projects, then Load more.\
- Project cards, hover previews and the project page layout follow the
references. Cover videos are muted, loop, and lazy-load with a poster
image.\
- Service pages show projects tagged with that service; hide the block
when there are none.\
- Each project page ends with a Next project link.\
\
CONTACT FORM\
Fields as in website-brief.md. Server action: validate, block spam with
a hidden honeypot field, save the submission to Sanity as an Enquiry,
email the company inbox via Resend, then show the success message. Start
a project buttons on service pages link to /contact?service=\<slug\> so
that service is pre-ticked.\
\
QUALITY (do not over-engineer)\
- Responsive at 375, 768, 1280 and 1440 widths; match mobile screenshots
where given\
- prefers-reduced-motion turns animations off\
- Title, description and share image per page and project from the CMS;
sitemap.xml; robots.txt blocking /studio\
- Images through next/image; aim for Lighthouse 90+ on mobile\
- Keyboard-usable menu, filters and form, with visible focus states\
\
COPY RULES (public site text)\
- Company name is Opsiogen. Never call it a \"team\". No other company
names, no tool or platform names, no personal or founder details, no
free offers.\
- The 7-day timeline applies to basic websites only; show that note on
the AI Websites & Revamps page.\
\
HOW TO WORK\
Build it end to end without stopping, except to ask me for: Sanity
project ID and API token, Resend API key and sending email address, and
the inbox that receives enquiries. Put them in .env.local and list them
in .env.example.\
Order: design tokens from the references \> CMS schemas and seed script
\> header, footer and pages \> animations \> contact form \> SEO.\
When done, run the site locally, compare each page against the
screenshots and fix differences. Then write a short README: how to run
it, how to deploy from GitHub, how to add a project, and how to edit
content in /studio.
